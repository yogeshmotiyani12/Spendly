Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address and a password (stored in hashed form). As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to keep the service running reliably.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate and improve Spendly: to authenticate you, to record and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you request. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database managed by us, and we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data and expense records, except where we are required by law to retain them.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may send a request to Google when you visit the site. We do not otherwise share your personal data with third parties, except where required by law or to protect the security of the service. If we add other services in the future, we will update this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or want to access, correct or delete your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
